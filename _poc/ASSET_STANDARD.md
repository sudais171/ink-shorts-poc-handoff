# Ink Shorts asset standard (from the Round 4 barrel)

What makes the barrel work, written so every new prop can be built to the same level.

## 1. Silhouette
- One shape you can name from its outline alone (barrel = bulging drum with bands).
- A gentle, deliberate exaggeration of the thing's defining feature (the barrel's bulge is ~13% wider at the middle than
  at the ends - more than a real barrel, less than a caricature).
- Chunky: structural parts are about 1.3-1.6x their real thickness, so they read at phone size.

## 2. Built from real parts
- The object is assembled from the parts a carpenter or smith would make: staves, hoops, lid planks, a bung.
- Parts are separate pieces with small gaps (2-4% of the part's width). The gaps catch the outline and shadow and
  explain how the thing is made. No painted-on seams.
- Every part has a job; no decoration that does not belong to the construction.

## 3. Edges
- All edges rounded: bevel about 1.5-2.5% of the object's height, 3 segments, so highlights and outlines sit on soft
  edges instead of razor corners.
- Larger structural parts get larger bevels than small ones (consistent look at every scale).

## 4. Materials and colour
- At most three materials per prop: a main material in two close tones that alternate between parts (light wood /
  slightly darker wood), plus one contrasting material (dark iron, brass, wax).
- Clear value contrast between them (light warm wood against near-black blue iron). The contrast material carries
  the controlled highlight; wood stays matte.
- Colours are mid-saturated and warm for wood; the contrast metal is cool.

## 5. Surface detail
- Detail comes from construction (gaps, plank seams, bands, pegs), not from texture noise.
- Wood carries only a few calm grain lines and soft tonal variation. No scribbles, no photo grain, no wear.

## 6. Balance
- Simple big forms first; a small number of deliberate details (barrel: 16 staves, 4 hoops, lid planks, one bung).
- If a detail is not readable at 1080x1920 from the usual camera distance, leave it out.
